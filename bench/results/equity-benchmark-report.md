### Key Findings

**Q1 — Does fusion beat the individuals?**  Fusion avg AUC = 0.5588, Sharpe = +0.06. Best individual by AUC: **AWM (rule regime)** (0.5737). Best individual by Sharpe: **XGBoost** (+2.07).  Δ(AUC) = -0.0149 → ❌ Fusion loses on AUC.

**Q2 — At what training N does fusion's advantage emerge?**  Fusion does not clearly beat the best individual at any threshold tested.

**Q3 — Is there a tradeable edge (Sharpe > 1.0)?**  ✅ Yes. Best: **XGBoost** at threshold=2500, Sharpe=+2.33, total return=702.02%. 20 (model, threshold) combinations cleared the bar.

### Recommendations

- **Highest AUC overall**: AWM (rule regime) at threshold=500 (AUC=0.5835). Use this combination if calibration / ranking is the primary goal.

- **Highest Sharpe**: XGBoost at threshold=2500 (Sharpe=+2.33). Use this for live trading allocation.

- Validate the fusion result on held-out 2025-2026 data before sizing positions.

- Re-run with `cost_bps=25` to stress-test edge resilience under realistic transaction costs.
